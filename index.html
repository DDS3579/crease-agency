4 stages / 3 services / 4 projects

Stage 1 of 4

Find the first crease.

Before anything is drawn, we decide what the site is for. One decision, made early, shapes everything after it.

Stage 2 of 4

Fold it into form.

Structure, type and motion are shaped together, as one material, until the page has a shape of its own.

Stage 3 of 4

Make it stand up.

Fast, accessible and easy to edit. The flat page becomes something you can use.

Stage 4 of 4

Unfold it to the world.

We ship it, watch how it behaves, and keep tuning. Then it is yours to run.
